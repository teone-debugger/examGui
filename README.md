### What is Dungeons and Dragons?
___Dungeons and Dragons___ is **not** only a text adventure. <br>
You can play our adventure RPG from Terminal or you can play with the Graphic option. <br>
**You** can choose from three different classes and three different races, pick up potions and much more from the dungeon, and even weapons but you have to make sure if your class can use that weapon ;) <br>
Pay attention to the various enemies in the dungeon, and don't understimate the power of the Coconuts. <br>

___Are you going to defeat the mighty Aurelion Sol?___

---
### How to Download and Install
***Clone the repository or download the .zip from GitHub***

***Windows***
- Download and extract the .zip file and extract the "examGui-main" folder on your desktop <br>
- Then open the cmd and compile using Maven by writing "mvn clean compile" and "mvn exec:java" <br>
- ___Make sure you're in the right folder before compiling___

***MacOs***
- Download and extract the .zip file and extract the "examGui-main" folder on your desktop <br>
- Then open "Finder" and go to "Applications" > "Utilities" and double-click "Terminal" <br>
- ___Make sure you're in the right folder before compiling___
